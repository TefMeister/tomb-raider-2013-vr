# The Epic sign-in prompt is a known 2021 change, and it has two documented ways round it

**Status:** 🆕 new · **Priority:** high for the first live run — it answers the board's first `[FLAT]`
row ("watching for any Epic Online Services sign-in prompt").

## What happened publicly

In **October 2021** the rebooted Tomb Raider trilogy (2013, Rise, Shadow) moved its multiplayer to
**Epic Online Services** `[reported 2026-10-11, Gamewatcher news story + Steam discussion]`. That is
why the 2013 executable carries EOS as a hard import (108 functions, dossier).

- Players whose **Steam and Epic accounts were linked** got a browser sign-in pop-up at start. Some
  reported that **cancelling it left them unable to reach the title screen**.
- **Nixxes** answered that a linked account is no longer used automatically: the feature can be turned
  on in the multiplayer options, or the accounts unlinked. They also put the **previous build** up as a
  **Steam beta branch** `[reported]`.
- Players who unlinked their accounts said saves loaded without trouble `[reported]`.

## What it means for our first launch

1. If a browser pop-up appears, do **not** cancel it blind: per the reports, cancelling can strand the
   game before the title screen.
2. The cleanest route is an account with **no Steam↔Epic link** — check that before the run.
3. If the prompt still blocks, the **pre-EOS beta branch** is the fallback. ⚠️ Our addresses and the
   108-function import count were measured on the current build; a beta build would need re-checking
   before any of our files go in.

No search found a newer (2024+) change to this `[checked 2026-10-11, one search]`.
